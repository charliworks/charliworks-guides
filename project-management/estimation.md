# Estimation

> Last reviewed: 2026-11 · Applies to: all projects · Status: 🚧 Draft

## TL;DR (the rules)

- Estimate issues in **T-shirt sizes**: `S` (≤ half a day), `M` (1–2 days), `L` (3–5 days). Anything bigger than `L` gets split.
- Estimate milestones as a range ("2–3 weeks"), never as a single date.
- After each milestone, compare estimates with what actually happened and note the ratio in the retro.
- For client work, quote using your *measured* ratio, plus a buffer ([client-work](../business/client-work.md)).

## Why

Solo estimates tend to be optimistic. Tracking the ratio makes them more honest over time.
