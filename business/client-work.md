# Client work kit

> Last reviewed: 2026-11 · Status: 🚧 Draft · Not legal advice

Use this when charliworks builds for a client.

## Flow

Inquiry → Discovery call → Proposal → Contract + deposit → Build (milestones) → Handoff → Support.

## Proposal (outline)

1. The client's problem, in their words
2. Proposed solution and **scope**: in and explicitly out
3. Milestones and deliverables
4. Timeline as a range ([estimation](../project-management/estimation.md))
5. Price and payment schedule (e.g. deposit / milestone / final)
6. Assumptions: what the client provides (content, access, feedback turnaround)
7. Revisions included, and how extra changes are priced
8. Validity date

## Contract must cover

- Scope reference (the approved proposal)
- Payment terms and late fees
- **IP ownership**: when it transfers (usually on final payment) and what you keep (reusable tools, the right to show it in your portfolio)
- Confidentiality and personal-data handling ([data-privacy](../market-ph/data-privacy.md))
- Warranty or bug-fix period, then the support rate
- Termination terms

## Change requests

Anything outside the scope gets a short written change request with its cost and timeline impact, approved **before** you do the work.

## Handoff checklist

- [ ] Code transferred to the client's repo or organization
- [ ] Credentials transferred, and your own access removed or documented ([infrastructure-inventory](../engineering/infrastructure-inventory.md))
- [ ] README and runbook complete
- [ ] Walkthrough session recorded
- [ ] Final invoice sent
