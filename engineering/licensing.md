# Licensing

> Last reviewed: 2026-11 · Applies to: all repos and assets

Not legal advice. Check an unusual license with a professional.

## Our code

- **Open source**: use MIT unless there's a reason not to. Write an ADR if you pick something else.
- **Closed source / client work**: no `LICENSE` file, or a proprietary notice. Client ownership terms are set in the contract ([client-work](../business/client-work.md)).

## Dependencies

- ✅ Usually fine for commercial use: MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, ISC.
- ⚠️ Check first: LGPL, MPL.
- ❌ Avoid in closed-source products unless you understand the obligations: GPL, AGPL.

## Fonts, images, icons, audio, game assets

- Keep a `CREDITS.md` or `assets/LICENSES.md` per project listing each asset, its source, license, and attribution requirement.
- Confirm commercial use is allowed. "Free" often means free for personal use only.
- AI-generated assets: record the tool used, and check that tool's terms for commercial use.
