# UI/UX principles

> Last reviewed: 2026-11 · Applies to: all products with a UI

1. **One primary action per screen.** Make it obvious.
2. **Design every state**: empty, loading, error, partial, and full. A good empty state also shows what to do next.
3. **Instant feedback.** Every tap or click responds within 100 ms, even if it's just a pressed state.
4. **Forgive mistakes.** Offer undo instead of confirmation dialogs wherever possible.
5. **Plain language.** Labels say what happens: "Save service record", not "Submit". See [brand-voice](brand-voice.md).
6. **Respect thumbs.** Touch targets are at least 44×44 px, and primary actions sit within thumb reach on mobile.
7. **Works offline or fails gracefully.** Connections drop. Tell the user what happened and what still works.
8. **Don't ask for data you don't need.** It's better UX and required by the [privacy rules](../market-ph/data-privacy.md).
9. **Test with real people** before calling a flow finished. Even two users help.
