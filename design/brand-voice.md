# Brand voice

> Last reviewed: 2026-11 · Applies to: READMEs, app copy, release notes, site, social

## How charliworks sounds

- **Clear before clever.** Say what it does.
- **Warm, not cute.** Friendly and human, with no forced jokes in error messages.
- **Confident, not hype.** Avoid words like "revolutionary", "seamless", "next-gen", "unleash".
- **Short.** If a sentence works without a word, cut the word.

## Examples

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| "Oops! Something went wrong 😢" | "Couldn't save. Check your connection and try again." |
| "Unleash the power of seamless tracking" | "Know when your car needs service." |
| "Submit" | "Save record" |

## Language

- Default product copy is English. If a product supports Filipino or Taglish, follow [localization](../market-ph/localization-payments.md).
- Release notes are written for users: "You can now…", not "Refactored X".
